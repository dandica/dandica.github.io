CASE STUDY / 01 · FOUR-PERSON UC DAVIS TEAM

Machine Learning for Underwater Gate Localization

Can a robot estimate a gate's depth and orientation from four detected image corners, without the original image?

View team repository ↗

My contributions

  • Helped prepare and validate 3,007 simulated observations, including investigating negative coordinates that proved to be valid partially out-of-frame views.
  • Helped engineer geometric features from four gate corners and compare linear regression, random forest, and neural-network approaches.
  • Contributed to held-out evaluation, cross-validation, robustness testing, and interpretation of model limitations.
ANALYTICAL JUDGMENT

Depth was predictable from the available inputs; orientation was not. Our team recommended against using the unreliable yaw estimator rather than overstating what the data could support.
